Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 6, 2026

At Digitme.fun, we take your privacy very seriously. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you visit our site digitme.fun and use our applications, including the Reverse app.

In summary: we do not collect any personal data.

1 Data Collected

We do not collect any personal data.

Our site does not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics tools or any other form of data collection. We do not store your IP address, browser information or any identifiable data.

2 Our Applications

Reverse App

The Reverse app was designed with your privacy in mind:

  • No data collection: we do not collect, store or transmit any of your personal data.
  • No account required: you do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.
  • Audio on your device: all voice recordings are processed and stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.
  • No advertising: the app contains no ads or advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics tools: we do not use any analytics or tracking tools within the app.

3 Microphone Permission

The Reverse app requires access to your device's microphone to record audio. This permission is used only for the app's core functionality — recording the sounds you choose to record. Audio data is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never transmitted over the Internet
  • Never shared with third parties

4 Third-Party Services

Our site and apps do not integrate any third-party services that collect personal data. We do not use:

  • • Google Analytics or any analytics platform
  • • Advertising networks (AdMob, Facebook Ads, etc.)
  • • Social media tracking pixels
  • • Third-party login services

5 Children's Privacy

Our apps are suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data, our apps can be used safely by children. We comply with applicable regulations relating to the protection of minors' data (GDPR, COPPA).

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
