How it works

Simple rules.
Surprisingly hard.

1

Memorize

A random shape appears on screen. You have a few seconds to study its outline before it disappears.

2

Redraw

Drag the vertices to recreate the shape from memory. No guide, no trace — just you and your recall.

3

Score

See your drawing overlaid on the original. Each shape is scored out of 10. Five shapes per round.
